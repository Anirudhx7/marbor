## Individual Contributor License Agreement

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Marbor (the "Project"). In order to clarify the intellectual property license granted with contributions from individuals or entities, the Project Owner requires that contributors accept this Contributor License Agreement ("Agreement").

This Agreement protects both you and the Project. It clarifies how contributions may be used, modified, and redistributed, while ensuring you retain copyright ownership in your original contributions.

This Agreement operates alongside the project's requirement for Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) sign-off. Contributors must continue to sign off every commit with `git commit -s`, certifying their right to submit the code.

This Agreement does not replace the requirement for Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) sign-off. You agree to sign off on every commit submitted to the Project (`git commit -s`), certifying that:
